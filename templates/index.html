Character Name Pip (Sweet baby age)

Section 03 · Origin Story

Pip’s Bedtime Journey

Follow Pip through the four quiet scenes of a night at Midnight Nursery (under 150 words per scene).

1
7:00 PM · Dusk Waking

The Nursery Awakes

While human children yawn and brush their teeth, Pip wakes up in a cloud hammock. Pip ties the starry nightcap, gives a tiny crooked smile to the moon, and brews warm chamomile stardust for the long night ahead.

2
9:30 PM · The Craft

Winding the Music Boxes

In the Lullaby Lab, Pip tests every music box with tiny fingers. If a note sounds too sharp, Pip softens it with lavender oil. Each song must feel like being wrapped in a warm flannel blanket fresh from the dryer.

3
12:00 AM · Midnight Flight

Across the Chimneys

With a cloud tucked under each arm, Pip floats over neighborhood rooftops. Through open attic windows, Pip slips dreams of strawberry meadows, gentle rains, and floating balloons into children’s sleepy ears.

4
5:30 AM · Morning Slumber

Pip’s Own Goodnight

As the horizon blushes pink, Pip returns home to Midnight Nursery. Satisfied that every creature is fast asleep, Pip curls into the soft cloud, closes the glowing amber eyes, and drifts into a dream of their own.
